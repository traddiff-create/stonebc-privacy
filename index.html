Privacy Policy

Stone Bicycle Coalition iOS App — Effective April 1, 2026

Overview

Stone Bicycle Coalition ("the App") is a free community app for cyclists. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account creation or login required
  • No personal information collected (name, email, phone)
  • No location tracking or GPS data stored
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No cookies or advertising identifiers
  • No data shared with third parties

Rally Radio (Voice Chat)

Rally Radio uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework for peer-to-peer voice chat between nearby devices. Audio is transmitted directly between devices on the local network and is never recorded, stored, or sent to any server. No audio data leaves your local network.

The App requests microphone access solely for Rally Radio voice chat. You can deny this permission and still use all other features.

Local Network Access

The App requests local network access to discover nearby devices for Rally Radio. This uses Apple's Bonjour protocol and does not involve any external servers.

Data Storage

All app content (bike listings, routes, events, community posts) is bundled within the app. No data is stored on external servers. If WordPress sync is enabled by the app operator, the app fetches publicly available content from a WordPress website — no user data is sent.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The App contains no user-generated content, accounts, or data collection mechanisms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
